## Description
Joshu brings your whole day into one calm place: what's due, what's on your calendar, the habits you're building, where your money is going and the book you're reading. Open it in the morning and you know exactly where you stand.

YOUR DAY ON ONE SCREEN
• Today shows what's due, what's next on your calendar, your habits and your budget at a glance
• Plan tomorrow in a minute: carry over what's left and pick your top three
• Week in review: what you got done and how the week felt

TASKS AND CALENDAR
• Add a task, event, expense or note in one line
• Every calendar on your iPhone and your Apple Reminders, next to your tasks
• Reminders at a time, or when you arrive at or leave a place

HABITS AND FOCUS
• Build habits with streaks and weekly targets
• Focus timer with a live countdown on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Focus filters: during Work Focus, Joshu shows only work

MONEY
• A monthly budget and what's left to spend
• Bills and subscriptions, with what's due next
• Log card payments and bank texts with the Shortcuts that come with Joshu, and scan receipts

READING, DREAMS AND MORE
• A bookshelf with your reading time and yearly goal
• A bucket list, trips and the places you want to go
• Notes, a journal with your mood, ideas and documents
• Insights: spending, focus, reading, habits and mood over time
• A digital business card with a QR code and Apple Wallet pass

ON YOUR HOME SCREEN AND EVERYWHERE
• Widgets you can tap to tick off habits and tasks, including on the Lock Screen
• Start focus and Add to Joshu in Control Center and on the Action button
• Siri and Shortcuts
• Your account keeps everything in sync with Joshu on your Mac

JOSHU PRO
Upgrade for your own AI assistant, named by you. Ask anything, plan your week, write and get things done, by voice or text, or hands-free by saying "Hey" and its name. Pro also adds a morning brief, a summary of your day, a money coach and smart capture that sorts one line into tasks, events and spending.

Joshu Pro is $7.99 a month or $59.99 a year. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

## App Review Information › Notes
Joshu works without an account; Sign in with Apple (or email) creates a free account that syncs between devices.
Joshu Pro (the AI assistant) is an auto-renewable subscription; please buy it with a sandbox account to try the
assistant, morning brief and money coach. Health access is read-only and optional (steps, sleep, workouts on the
Insights screen). Location is used only for weather and place-based reminders you set.
