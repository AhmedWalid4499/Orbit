Install in a minute

  1. Download Joshu.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Joshu into your Applications folder.
  3. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't check Joshu, because it isn't from the App Store. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

After that, Joshu keeps itself up to date: a button appears in the top bar when a new version is out.

On Android

  1. Download Joshu.apk on your phone and open it.
  2. Android asks to allow installing apps from your browser. Allow it once, then tap Install.
  3. Sign in with your Joshu account and everything from your Mac comes across.

Questions

Is Joshu free?

Yes. Joshu Free has everything except the AI: tasks, calendar, habits, money, reading, ideas, notes, sync across your devices and one email account. Joshu Pro ($7.99 a month or $59.99 a year) adds your own AI assistant (you give it a name), plus morning briefs, email summaries, the day summary, the money coach, LinkedIn job alerts and as many email accounts as you like.

Do I need an API key?

No. With Joshu Pro the AI just works: Joshu takes care of it. There's nothing to set up.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 15 Sequoia or newer.

When is the iPhone app coming?

Soon. It's built and in testing now. Use Joshu on your Mac today and sign in to sync; everything will be there on your iPhone when it launches.

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

Joshu is distributed outside the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You won't be asked again, and updates install by themselves.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. A Windows version is on the way, with your data synced from your Mac and phone.
