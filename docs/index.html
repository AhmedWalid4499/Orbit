✦ Your assistant

Ask anything. It actually does it.

Talk to Orbit's assistant like a friend who knows your calendar, tasks, money and books. It sets things up for you, and every change has an Undo.

  • Acts for you. “Remind me to call mom tomorrow at 6” becomes a task. “Help me read more” becomes a habit.
  • Send it files. Drop in a PDF, photo or Word document. It reads the bill, ticket or letter and adds the dates and to-dos.
  • Talk out loud. Tap the mic, hear replies, and keep the conversation going hands-free.
  • Knows the world. Finds food and places near you, checks prices and reviews, and tells you what to wear from the forecast.
☾ Plan tomorrow

End the day with a plan, not a pile.

Each evening, Orbit helps you clear what's left and pick tomorrow's top three. Just say how tomorrow looks, and the assistant moves things around, adds tasks and saves your plan.

  • Your top 3 waits at the top of Today in the morning, and the morning brief starts from it.
  • Focus timer for 15, 25 or 50 minutes on any task, counting down in the menu bar.
  • Morning brief with a headline for your day, three focus items and a heads-up.
◎ Money

Know where your money goes, without typing it in.

Orbit fills in your spending for you and helps you keep more of it.

  • Receipts from email become expenses by themselves, in any currency.
  • Bank statements (CSV or PDF) import in one go, with duplicates skipped.
  • Subscriptions with monthly and yearly totals, price-rise alerts and a nudge before a free trial starts charging.
  • Budgets per category and a money coach with savings tips based on your real spending.

Install in a minute

  1. Download Orbit.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Orbit into your Applications folder.
  3. The first time you open it, macOS may say it can't check Orbit, because it isn't from the App Store. Go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

After that, Orbit keeps itself up to date: a button appears in the top bar when a new version is out.

Yours, and private

Orbit keeps your data on your Mac and works offline. If you turn on sync, each account can only ever see its own rows.

  • Keys stay in the Keychain, never in files.
  • End-to-end encrypted key sync with your own passphrase, so the server only stores ciphertext.
  • Bring your own Claude key. AI features talk to Anthropic directly from your Mac, with your own API key.

Questions

Is Orbit free?

Yes, Orbit is free to download and use. The AI features use your own Anthropic API key, so Anthropic bills you directly for what you use.

Do I need an API key?

Only for the AI features: the assistant, the morning brief, planning by conversation, receipts from email and the money coach. Tasks, calendar, habits, money, reading, notes and everything else work without one. Add a key any time in Settings › AI.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 15 Sequoia or newer.

When is the iPhone app coming?

Soon. It's built and in testing now. Use Orbit on your Mac today and sign in to sync; everything will be there on your iPhone when it launches.

Why does macOS warn me the first time?

Orbit is distributed outside the App Store, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You won't be asked again, and updates install by themselves.

Is there a Windows version?

Yes, there's an early Windows version with your data synced from the Mac. Download Orbit-Setup.exe.
