Questions

Do I need my own AI or API key?

No. With Pro the AI just works: Joshu takes care of it, on all your devices.

What does Free leave out?

Only the AI: the assistant, morning brief, day summary, email summaries, money coach and LinkedIn job alerts. Free can also connect one email account; Pro connects as many as you like. Everything else is in Free.

Is there a limit?

Pro is for one person's everyday use, with a generous daily fair-use limit that normal use doesn't come close to.

Does Pro work on my Mac and my iPhone?

Yes. Buy it once and it works everywhere you're signed in to your Joshu account.

How do I cancel?

Any time, in your Apple ID's subscription settings. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then Joshu goes back to Free. Nothing you made is deleted.

What happens to my data?

When you ask the AI something, what it needs to answer is sent to the AI to make the reply, and isn't used to train it. More in our privacy policy.
