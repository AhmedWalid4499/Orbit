Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Joshu is a personal organiser. Your data is yours: Joshu doesn't sell it, doesn't show ads and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, calendar, habits, money, books, ideas, notes and everything else you add are stored on your device. Joshu works offline.

Sync

If you sign in to a Joshu account, your data is synced between your devices through our database provider (Supabase). Each account can only ever read its own data. Your sign-ins to other services (such as Google) are encrypted before they leave your device.

The AI (Joshu Pro)

When you use Yuki, the morning brief, email summaries, the day summary or the money coach, the information needed to answer (for example your question, today's events and tasks, or the emails being summarised) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API through Joshu's server to produce the reply. Joshu's server checks your subscription and counts requests for fair use; it doesn't keep the content of your requests. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models. If you choose the optional natural voice, the text being read aloud is sent to OpenAI to make the audio.

Email, calendar and other connections

Connections are optional and you can disconnect any of them in Settings › Connections. When you connect Gmail, Joshu reads your recent emails on your device to summarise them, find receipts and job alerts, and lets you reply. Joshu's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendars, reminders, Health, contacts and location are read through Apple's permissions, only when you allow them, and stay on your device unless you sync.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Joshu receives a signed confirmation of your subscription (not your payment details) to unlock Pro on your devices.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything in Settings › General › Reset, disconnect any service in Settings › Connections, and delete your Joshu account and its synced data from Settings › Account.

Children

Joshu isn't directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue, or use the contact link on Joshu's App Store page.
